🔐 Password Security Guide

Learn how to protect your accounts from hackers and attacks

Strong Password Rules

Use a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Example: CyB3r@Safe2026!

Weak vs Strong Passwords

Weak: 123456 or password
Strong: Ug@ndaSecure#2026

Multi-Factor Authentication

Adds an extra layer of security by requiring OTP codes or app verification after password entry.

Password Managers

Tools like password vaults help generate and store strong passwords securely without memorizing them.

Why It Matters

Most hacks happen because of weak passwords. Strong passwords reduce risk by over 80%.

Pro Tip

Never reuse passwords across multiple accounts. If one is hacked, all accounts become vulnerable.
