Threat Landscape

Common cyber threats users face online

Cyber threats affect both individuals and organisations. Understanding how these threats work makes it easier to prevent them and respond early.

Phishing

Attackers send fake messages or create imitation websites to trick users into revealing passwords, PINs, or account details.

Malware

Malicious software can damage devices, steal files, monitor activity, or open unauthorised access to a system.

Ransomware

Files or systems may be locked and users are pressured to pay money to regain access to their data.

Identity Theft

Personal details can be stolen and used to impersonate victims, access accounts, or commit fraud.

Awareness Mission

Promoting digital responsibility and safer online behaviour

CyberSafe Uganda encourages a culture of cyber awareness by sharing reliable information, preventive tips, and practical examples of digital risks. The goal is to help users make informed choices and reduce vulnerability to cybercrime.

  • Encourage safe password habits and account protection
  • Reduce successful phishing and scam attempts
  • Promote privacy awareness on social media and messaging platforms
  • Support safe use of devices, browsers, and public internet connections
  • Help students and communities understand digital responsibility
